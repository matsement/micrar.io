Micrar.io — Become a microbe

This game is a provotype for designers who want to explore what it means to “become microbes.” In biodesign, living organisms like bacteria, fungi and algae are often treated like materials, but microbes are difficult to understand because they are too small, operate at unfamiliar timescales, and sense the world in ways humans cannot directly experience. This prototype invites you to think from the microbe’s perspective and to notice what a designer might learn by shifting attention away from human-centred control.

The idea is not to replace lab work, but to complement it with digital, immersive ways of imagining microbial life. By changing size, time and movement, the game explores six microbial qualities: motility, scales, rhythms, translucency, communication and biophysics.
